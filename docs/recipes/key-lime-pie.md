# Key Lime Pie

A classic Florida dessert featuring a creamy, tart key lime filling in a sweet graham cracker crust, topped with whipped cream.

## Information

- **Prep Time**: 20 minutes
- **Chill Time**: 3 hours
- **Total Time**: 3 hours 20 minutes
- **Servings**: 8 slices
- **Difficulty**: Easy

## Ingredients

### Graham Cracker Crust
- 1½ cups graham cracker crumbs (about 10-12 graham crackers)
- ⅓ cup granulated sugar
- 6 tablespoons butter, melted

### Key Lime Filling
- 4 large egg yolks
- 1 (14 oz) can sweetened condensed milk
- ½ cup fresh key lime juice (about 12-15 key limes)
- 1 tablespoon key lime zest
- Pinch of salt

### Topping
- 1 cup heavy whipping cream
- 2 tablespoons powdered sugar
- ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
- Lime slices for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. **Preheat oven** to 350°F (175°C).

2. **Make the crust**: In a medium bowl, combine graham cracker crumbs, sugar, and melted butter. Mix until the mixture resembles wet sand.

3. **Press the crust**: Press the mixture firmly into the bottom and up the sides of a 9-inch pie pan. Use the bottom of a measuring cup to pack it down evenly.

4. **Bake the crust**: Bake for 10 minutes until lightly golden. Remove from oven and let cool slightly. Reduce oven temperature to 325°F (165°C).

5. **Make the filling**: In a large bowl, whisk together egg yolks and lime zest until tinted light green (about 2 minutes).

6. **Add condensed milk**: Whisk in sweetened condensed milk until smooth.

7. **Add lime juice**: Slowly whisk in key lime juice and salt until well combined. The mixture will begin to thicken.

8. **Fill the crust**: Pour the filling into the pre-baked graham cracker crust.

9. **Bake the pie**: Bake at 325°F for 15-17 minutes until the filling is just set but still slightly jiggly in the center.

10. **Cool**: Let the pie cool to room temperature on a wire rack, then refrigerate for at least 3 hours or overnight.

11. **Make whipped cream**: Just before serving, beat heavy cream, powdered sugar, and vanilla in a chilled bowl until stiff peaks form.

12. **Serve**: Top the chilled pie with whipped cream and garnish with lime slices if desired.

## Tips

!!! tip "Pro Tips"
    - If key limes aren't available, regular Persian limes work too
    - Don't overbake - the filling should still have a slight jiggle
    - For cleaner slices, dip your knife in hot water between cuts
    - The pie can be frozen for up to 2 months
    - Use fresh lime juice, not bottled, for best flavor

## Variations

- **Meringue topping**: Replace whipped cream with Italian or Swiss meringue, then torch until golden
- **No-bake version**: Skip baking the filling and chill for 4-6 hours instead
- **Chocolate crust**: Replace graham crackers with chocolate cookie crumbs

## Storage

Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. The crust may soften slightly over time.
